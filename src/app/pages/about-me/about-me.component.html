About Me.

My name is Noah and I enjoy designing and building things that live on the internet. My interest in coding started back in 2018 when I wrote a program that prints "hello world" in C — turns out telling the computer what to do and then watching it actually do it got me completely hooked.

Over the years I've had the privilege of working with some great teams across a range of industries — Banking, FinTech, EdTech, E-commerce, and Marketplaces. That variety has shaped me into a backend engineer who's equally at home building financial APIs, real-time messaging systems, exam platforms at scale, or marketplace services — my backend experience goes well beyond fintech.

These days I spend most of my time building reliable, well-structured backend systems and APIs, while also bringing solid full-stack experience to the front-end side of things. As a software engineer, I specialise in building incredible digital experiences, and occasionally designing them too.

Aside from sitting in front of my machine, I love playing video games, drumming, and consuming shawarma and ice-cream in unhealthy quantities.
